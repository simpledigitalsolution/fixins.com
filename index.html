How it works

From "what's for dinner?" to dinner.

Scroll through the real app. Flip the look any time.

1

Plan for everyone at the table

Set how many people you're feeding and give each person their own diet and restrictions. One plan, with variants that fit everyone, so nobody gets a separate dinner by accident.

  • Day, week or month plans
  • Per-person diet variants
  • Save plans and switch between them
2

Tell it what you've got

Fixins keeps a running pantry, so plans and lists work around what's already at home. Add things however is quickest.

  • Type it in, or tap quick-add chips
  • Scan a grocery receipt
  • Snap a photo of the pantry shelf
  • "Cook from pantry" when you don't want to shop
3

A shopping list that writes itself

Your list is built from the recipes in your plan, grouped by aisle, minus what's already in the pantry. Share it with whoever's doing the run.

  • Built from your plan's recipes
  • Price estimates from nearby stores
  • Check things off as you go
4

Cook one step at a time

Big, readable steps you can follow with flour on your hands. Timers when a step needs one, and safe cooking temperatures a tap away.

  • Step-by-step cook mode
  • Built-in step timers
  • Safe cooking temperature guide
5

Let the house vote

Anyone in the household can suggest a dish and give meals a thumbs up or down. Fewer "I don't care, you pick" conversations.

  • Household dish suggestions
  • Votes for breakfast, lunch and dinner
  • Invite the people you cook for
6

Four looks. Pick yours.

Warm painted-food Classic, clean Redesign, dark Inverted with an acid-green accent, or Editorial. Switch in Settings and it changes right away.

  • Classic
  • Redesign
  • Inverted
  • Editorial

About Fixins™

Built for the kitchen counter, not the app store.

Fixins™ started with one question: why is planning dinner still the hardest part of family life? We built it to turn that nightly stress into a simple system that works for real households, not ideal schedules.

If planning is easier, food gets better. Better food means more energy, less waste, and calmer evenings for everyone at the table.
